My best trip was hiking in the, um, South Island of New Zealand with a couple friends. We did a mix. We spent a few days around Queenstown. Then we drove to the mountain, Mount Cook. We ended with a shorter track because we didn't want to carry those big heavy bags for a week.

The scenery was incredible. The blue lakes, the glacial lakes looked fake. There was wind hitting you from the side. And there were these quiet places where you only hear your feet. The best part—the highlight—was getting up early. We made coffee, that instant kind, in the cold. We watched the light change on the mountains. We weren't looking for big thrills or anything. It was more like moving all day and eating a lot. Then we would sleep hard and do it again. On the drive days we would stop at the lookouts, the random viewpoints, and just sit there. It is the trip I think about whenever I feel, ah, burnt out.
