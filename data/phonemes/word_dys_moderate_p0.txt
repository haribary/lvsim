My best trip was—it was hiking in New Zealand. The southern part. I went with a couple of friends. We did different things. We stayed a few days near that place, near Queenstown. Then we drove to the mountain. The tall mountain there. We ended with a shorter walk. A long walk but for a few days. None of us wanted to carry the heavy bags. The big packs for a whole week.

The scenery was—it was so good. The lakes were very blue. Like the ice water. They looked fake. The wind hits you from the side. It hits you sideways. And the quiet places. You just hear your feet. Your walking on the ground. The best part was getting up early. Making the coffee. The quick kind. You make it in the cold and watch the light. The light on the mountains. We weren't looking for—for the excitement. That fast stuff. It was just moving. Moving and eating and sleeping. On the drive days we stopped. We stopped at the places to look at the view. We just sat there. It is the trip I think about when I feel—when I am very tired and done with things.
