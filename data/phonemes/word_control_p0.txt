My best trip was, uh, hiking in New Zealand's South Island with a couple friends. We we did a mix: a few days around Queenstown, then drove to Mount Cook, and ended with a shorter multi-day track because, you know, none of us wanted to carry huge packs for a week. The scenery was, um, ridiculous—glacial lakes that look fake, wind that hits you sideways, and well, these quiet stretches where you can hear nothing but your footsteps. The highlight was, uh, getting up early, making instant coffee in the cold, and watching the light change on the mountains. We weren't chasing adrenaline; it was, like, more like moving all day, eating a lot, sleeping hard, repeat. On the drive days, you know, we'd stop at random viewpoints and just sit there. It's the the trip I think about whenever I feel burnt out.
