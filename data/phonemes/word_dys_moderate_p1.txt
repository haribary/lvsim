When I was a kid, my—my grandparents used to take me to a—a little lake early in the morning. We would go before it got hot. We would bring a—a container for the tea and just sit on the—the wooden part, the dock, with our feet in the water.

My grandpa would show me the—the birds. He would tell me the names like it was—um, important news. I didn’t quite get it then, but I remember feeling very—very quiet and safe. There was nowhere else I needed to be. Even now, the smell of the water and the—the cream for the sun, it takes me right back.
