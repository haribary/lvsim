What I like about—about the place. Where I live. It is easy. You can have the day. The—the real day. You do not have to plan. To fix it before.

I can walk to get—to get the drink. The hot drink. And do the—the jobs. The things. And I go to the green place. The grass place. Or the path. On the—the later time. 

The areas. The streets. They have a—a way. They are all different. So a walk. A short walk. It is not the same. It depends on the—the way. Which way I go. 

I like that there is—there is things. Things going on. The food—the food places outside. The small things. But you do not have to—to do it. To be there. It is just there. If you want. If you want the thing.
